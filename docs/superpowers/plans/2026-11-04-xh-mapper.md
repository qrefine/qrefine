# X-H Mapper Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** qr.refine refines nuclear coordinates while fmodel (Fcalc, R factors, maps, data target and gradients) always sees X-ray X-H lengths for X-ray scattering tables, and the written model follows the scattering-table convention.

**Architecture:** A new `mmtbx.hydrogens.xh_mapper.mapper` holds, per bonded H, the parent index and the X-ray and nuclear ideal lengths, and provides `shrink`, `expand` and the chain-rule `gradients`. qrefine's `calculator.sites` owns the nuclear sites and keeps `fmodel.xray_structure` at `mapper.shrink(nuclear)`; `driver.refine` and `results.manager` pass nuclear sites explicitly and never read sites back from fmodel; `results.manager` applies the mapper when writing. `fit_rotatable2` learns to leave H lengths and riding alone.

**Tech Stack:** cctbx_project (mmtbx.hydrogens, mmtbx.model, scitbx flex), qrefine (calculator.py, driver.py, results.py, refine.py, qr.py, tests/unit).

**Spec:** `qrefine/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-04-xh-mapper-design.md` (umbrella: `qrefine/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-04-qrefine-in-phenix-refine-design.md`, D5, D6, D15b)
