==============================
Preparing a QM-ready model
==============================

.. contents::

Overview
--------

``qr.finalise`` prepares a model for Q|R refinement by running
``mmtbx.reduce2`` with ``qmready=True``: it adds hydrogens (water included),
uses neutron X-H distances, adds OXT and NH3+ at chain ends and breaks, checks
for clashes, calculates the total charge and writes ``modelH_charge<q>.pdb``
(the charge in the name). Truncated side chains must be rebuilt first; a
model capped with ``capping=True`` is for QM calculations only and cannot be
refined.

Running the program
-------------------

``qr.finalise model.pdb`` is ``mmtbx.reduce2 model.pdb qmready=True``. Any
other argument is passed to ``mmtbx.reduce2``.
